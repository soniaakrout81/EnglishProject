Lack of Resources

Description:
Some schools do not have enough books, computers, classrooms, or teachers. Overcrowded classes and lack of materials make learning difficult. Students may struggle to understand lessons or fall behind. Schools without proper resources also limit students’ ability to participate in sports, arts, and clubs.





















Results:
Around 272 million children and youth were out of school in 2023, partly due to a lack of resources. In poor countries, about 33% of older students are not in school, compared to only 3% in rich countries. Students in schools with few resources may feel frustrated, bored, and may perform poorly academically.






















Solutions:
Solutions: Governments can provide more funding to schools. Communities can help with
book drives, computer donations, or volunteer programs. Teachers can use creative
teaching methods to make the most of limited resources. Improving school resources
allows students to learn better, enjoy school, and have more opportunities for success.
